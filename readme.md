About Me
Hi, I'm Mohammad Munayam Sowdagor (RSM Monaem) — a software developer and project manager focused on turning complex requirements into reliable, maintainable products.

My work spans desktop applications, multi-tenant SaaS, AI integrations, ERP and accounting workflows, eCommerce systems, APIs, and deployment infrastructure. I enjoy working across the full delivery lifecycle: understanding the business problem, shaping the architecture, building the product, deploying it, and improving it through testing and performance analysis.
- 🧩 Product engineering: Desktop apps, SaaS platforms, web applications, and business automation.
- 🖥️ Systems & desktop: Rust, Tauri, TypeScript, and cross-platform application design.
- 🤖 AI engineering: Local LLM orchestration, model routing, API-compatible gateways, and inference integrations.
- 🏢 Business software: ERP workflows, accounting, inventory, POS, eCommerce, and reporting.
- ⚙️ Infrastructure: Linux servers, Docker, CI/CD, databases, caching, and production troubleshooting.
- 🤝 Collaboration: Technical planning, practical architecture, delivery coordination, and solving real business problems.
<details>
  <summary><strong>More about my engineering interests</strong></summary>
  <br />

  I'm especially interested in building software that stays understandable as it grows: clear service boundaries, well-designed data models, secure session handling, observable APIs, predictable deployments, and performance improvements backed by measurement rather than guesswork.

  I value pragmatic engineering: choose the simplest design that meets the requirements, protect user data, document important decisions, and keep the system maintainable for the people who will work on it next.
</details>
